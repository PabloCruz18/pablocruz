Professional case · 2025

LGV hydraulics — Recurring failure analysis

Durability and reliability investigation, fluid-power service and transferable marine mechanical diagnostics.

Engineering problem

Recurring hydraulic leaks affected 32 of approximately 60 guided vehicles. Initial repairs sometimes failed again within about a week.

My contribution

I tracked failures by unit, inspected and isolated leaks, replaced O-rings and completed four piston-seal repairs. I identified the common specification issue and supported implementation of the corrected O-rings.

Testing & verification

After repair I cycled the lift, inspected the pressurized connections, returned vehicles to operation and followed recurrence. I adapted an existing preventive maintenance plan to include inspection actions.

Results & limitations

30 of 32 affected units stopped exhibiting the recurring sealing issue; the remaining two had different mechanical faults. No quantified cost or downtime savings are claimed. This is operational reliability evidence, not a controlled endurance test.
