Academic team project · 2022

EV battery housing — Preliminary structural analysis

Vehicle structural development, simulation interpretation and prototype verification.

Engineering problem

The team developed a concept for an enclosure accommodating 36 battery modules while simplifying assembly.

My contribution

I modeled the lateral structure, conducted preliminary static and impact simulations, prepared material selection and DFA analyses, and contributed to the scale prototype.

Testing & verification

The static analysis used a total equivalent load of 4,233.6 N. I reviewed stresses and deformation; the impact analysis indicated localized deformation but also stresses above material yield.

Results & limitations

The team concept reduced theoretical part count from 17 to 12 and estimated assembly time from approximately 485 to 318 seconds. Simulations were preliminary, not correlated with full-scale testing; mesh refinement and further validation remained necessary.
