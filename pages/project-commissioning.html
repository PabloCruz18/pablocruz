Professional case · 2025–present

Industrial machinery — Commissioning & functional verification

Field commissioning, multidisciplinary system diagnostics and transferable marine equipment service.

Engineering problem

Automated plate-mounting and inspection equipment combines mechanical motion, pneumatics, vacuum, sensing and industrial vision.

My contribution

I personally installed and commissioned three machines and serviced more than 20 U.S. customer sites. I inspected alignment, reference positions, sensor response, camera operation and motion behavior.

Testing & verification

In representative cases I isolated pneumatic pressure loss and corrected configuration issues after a power outage, then verified operation after corrective action.

Results & limitations

At least 10 machines were restored through direct technical intervention across the broader service role. Exact downtime savings were not measured. I did not own major software development or complete machine design.
