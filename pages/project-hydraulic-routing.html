Professional internship · 2022–2023

Agricultural machinery — Hydraulic routing integration

Mechanical integration, vehicle packaging and design verification.

Engineering problem

Hydraulic pipes and hoses needed to fit within moving agricultural machinery assemblies while accounting for installation and serviceability.

My contribution

I developed routing concepts, CAD geometry and documentation using Creo and Windchill. I compared motion paths, interference, length and component reuse.

Testing & verification

Motion analysis supported a revised hose path; a reusable line concept was developed for three configurations of the same platform.

Results & limitations

The hose route was 13% shorter and the reusable line concept reduced line cost by 20%. Production release and full physical durability validation are not confirmed.
