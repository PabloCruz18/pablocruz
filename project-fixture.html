Academic team project · June 2024

Checking fixture — Measurement system validation

Measurement system analysis, instrumentation selection and interpretation of test variability.

Engineering problem

The team evaluated a datum-based checking fixture and manufacturing proposal for a machined component.

My contribution

My main contribution was fixture design, instrument selection, measurement and analysis of repeatability and reproducibility. I collaborated on the wider manufacturing and quality proposal.

Testing & verification

Gauge R&R results were 6.85% for the micrometer diameter, 16.41% for the vernier diameter, 23.18% for the LVDT diameter, 1.00% for LVDT warpage and 22.23% for laser length.

Results & limitations

The study identified measurement methods requiring improvement, rather than demonstrating universal acceptance. Some process capability results were also poor. This was an academic proposal, not a released production process.
